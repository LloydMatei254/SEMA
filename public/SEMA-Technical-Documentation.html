1. Executive Summary

Safaricom SEMA is Safaricom's centralized customer complaint ingestion, multimodal dialect AI triage, root cause clustering, and automated rapid dispatch management system. It bridges the gap between Kenyan subscribers articulating issues in conversational Kiswahili and Sheng and specialized engineering crews.

Core Objectives

  • Mean Time to Triage (MTTT): Reduced from 45 minutes to under 3 seconds using Google Gemini Multimodal Generative AI.
  • Zero-Friction Ingestion: Audio recording with instant phoneme recognition in English, Kiswahili, and Sheng.
  • Root Cause Clustering: Automatically aggregates dozens of duplicate complaints during local fiber cuts into a single master incident.

2. Complete System Architecture
